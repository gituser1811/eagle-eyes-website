OUR IDENTITY

Who We Are

Eagle Eyes Group of Companies supports businesses in Qatar by coordinating manpower requirements with international recruitment agencies and workforce suppliers.

Operating from Doha, we specialize in understanding exact employer specifications and coordinating with licensed recruitment agencies across Africa and Asia. Our focus is ensuring every placement meets regulatory standards, professional qualifications, and organizational fit.

Whether sourcing specialized healthcare practitioners, hospitality staff, culinary talent, skilled technicians, or essential support staff, Eagle Eyes facilitates the recruitment lifecycle with transparency, diligence, and structured international coordination.
